Section 1

Open House Prep Checklist

Run through this before, during, and after every event.

1–2 Days Before

  • Confirm event address, time, and agent partner with the listing agent
  • Print sign-in sheets and business cards (bring extra of each)
  • Know today's rate ranges for common programs so you can speak to payments on the spot
  • Charge tablet/phone for digital sign-in or pre-approval app demo
  • Review the property's price point so you can speak to likely loan programs and payment ranges

Marketing

  • Customize and print the Canva Open House Flyer — see Section 2
  • Post the Open House social media graphic to your channels 1–2 days before the event
  • Pull a fresh Pricing Scenario Flyer from Brand 360 (Content Creator) the morning of the event — don't reuse an old one
  • Confirm Easy Qualifier and Home Value Estimator are bookmarked and ready to demo on-site

Day Of

  • Arrive 15 minutes early to set up materials and coordinate with the agent
  • Bring coffee and donuts for the agent (and any co-hosting agents) — small gesture, big goodwill
  • Have sign-in sheet and consent language visible and easy to complete
  • Collect contact info for every visitor, even "just looking" traffic
  • Note financing type mentioned (cash, pre-approved, needs pre-approval, investor, etc.)

Within 24 Hours After

  • Log every lead in your CRM / lead tracker (see Section 6)
  • Send Day-1 follow-up email or text to all attendees (see Section 5)
  • Send the listing agent a thank-you note and a quick recap of leads generated
  • Schedule any pre-approval calls booked at the event
Remember: an open house is really about the relationship you build with the listing agent — the leads are a bonus. Coffee, donuts, and being genuinely useful to the agent will get you invited back long after any single lead converts.

Section 3

Buyer FAQ Handout

A leave-behind that answers the questions buyers ask most at open houses.

What's the difference between pre-qualified and pre-approved?

Pre-qualification is a quick, informal estimate based on self-reported information. Pre-approval involves verifying income, assets, and credit, and results in a conditional commitment — it's what sellers take seriously in a competitive offer.

How much down payment do I actually need?

It depends on the loan program: conventional loans can go as low as 3–5% down, FHA as low as 3.5%, and VA loans often require 0% down for eligible veterans. Down payment amount also affects mortgage insurance requirements.

What other costs should I budget for besides the down payment?

Closing costs typically run 2–5% of the loan amount and cover items like lender fees, title insurance, appraisal, and prepaid taxes/insurance. Ask your loan officer for a Loan Estimate early so there are no surprises.

How long does pre-approval take?

insert your typical turnaround time — most buyers can get a conditional pre-approval within a day or two of submitting income and asset documentation.

Can I still shop if my credit isn't perfect?

Yes — there are programs for a range of credit profiles. A loan officer can review your situation and outline what's available, and what steps would improve your rate or terms.

What happens after I make an offer?

Once an offer is accepted, your lender orders the appraisal, finalizes underwriting, and issues a Closing Disclosure at least three business days before closing. Your loan officer will walk you through each milestone.

Keep this general and educational — avoid quoting firm rates or terms on a leave-behind, since they change daily. Direct specific numbers to a one-on-one conversation.

Section 5

Follow-Up Scripts

Send within the timeframes below. Personalize the bracketed fields.

Same day — text or email
Hi Name, great meeting you at the open house on street name today! If you'd like a personalized payment estimate or want to get pre-approved before your next showing, I'm happy to jump on a quick call. What works best for you this week?
Day 3 — no response
Hi Name, just following up in case my last message got buried. Even if you're early in your search, getting pre-approved now means you can move fast when the right home shows up. Want me to send over a quick, no-obligation estimate?
Day 7 — nurture
Hi Name, wanted to check in — still house hunting? Rates and programs shift often, so I'm glad to send a fresh estimate whenever it's useful, no pressure. Let me know if you'd like me to keep you posted on options.
Buyer asked a financing question on-site
Hi Name, following up on the question you asked about topic — down payment/credit/etc.. Here's a quick answer: brief answer. Happy to go deeper on a short call if that's helpful.
Referral thank-you to listing agent
Hi Agent Name, thanks for having me at property address today. We picked up # leads, including standout lead, if any. I'll keep you posted as these move through pre-approval. Let me know the next event you'd like me at.
